FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still not sure where to start? Call or text and we’ll talk it through.

How do you price a job?

Every garage is different, so we quote from your photos, video or a short visit. Price depends on garage size, how much is leaving and which storage you choose. You get a written estimate before any work starts.

Do I have to get rid of my things?

No. You decide what stays. We help you sort, remove only what you choose to let go, then build storage around what you keep.

What happens to the items you remove?

We separate items for donation, recycling or disposal wherever possible. [Confirm donation and recycling partners.]

Can I book storage without a cleanout?

Yes. Tote System, Wall System and Floor Finish can be booked on their own, though most garages work best after a Caveback Reset.

Do I need to be home during the work?

We walk through the plan with you at the start and confirm what stays. After that you don’t need to be there the whole time.

Which areas do you serve?

Langley, Surrey, White Rock, Abbotsford, Maple Ridge and surrounding Lower Mainland communities. Not sure? Ask us.
